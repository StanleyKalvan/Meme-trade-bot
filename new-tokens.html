 📉 Pullback: buy the dip near the low of the window
 
Buys after a real drop, close to the lowest price of the look-back window (default 24 h, like the 1-day chart), once buyers return. It does not buy after a spike. Needs the proxy URL (Connection & keys). The token age window is in Token filters.

 
 

  

  

  

  

  

  

 











 🚀 Launch: new tokens climbing since release on Jupiter
 
Age is counted from the first pool on Jupiter. Buys the first healthy pullback in a token that keeps climbing (like Quantum Inu), never the spike. The riskiest style: most new tokens go to zero. Needs the proxy URL.

 
 

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

  

 

 
 
 
 
 











 ⚡ Momentum: buy strength
 
Buys tokens that are rising right now. Entries are usually close to the highs of a candle, so this style is not recommended. It does not need the proxy.

 
 

  

  

  

  

 











 👁 Watchlist dips: older tokens (30+ days)
 
Works alongside the entry style above. Buys named tokens (for example useless, fartcoin, doge) after a deep drop from the 90-day high, near the 30-day low. Needs the proxy URL.

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

  

  

  

  

  

  

 











 🛡 Risk & exits
 
Apply to every entry style. Dip and Launch entries set their own stop (just below the pullback low) and target, within these limits.
